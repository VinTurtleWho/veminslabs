veminsLab builds game-specific artificial intelligence for competitive multiplayer games. By recreating core mechanics in headless, high-throughput C++ engines, we train specialized models that discover novel strategies and show players what they could have done differently.

Our Methodology

The Three-Pillar Architecture

Pillar 01

Deterministic Simulation

Traditional game clients are bottlenecked by 3D rendering and heavy runtime loops. We reconstruct 1-to-1 headless C++ numerical engines of target games, enabling millions of game ticks per second for RL training.

Pillar 02

Specialized ML Architectures

Rather than brute-forcing generalized models, we build compact, game-specific architectures tailored to complex macro positioning, objective pacing, and unit mechanics.

Pillar 03

Stockfish-like Strategy Discovery

Our goal is post-match deep analysis: enabling players to evaluate tactical mistakes, test counterfactual decisions, and discover winning strategies discovered through rapid retraining.

First Research Target

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang

We are currently modeling 5v5 map dynamics, hero mechanics, ability interactions, and lane pacing in our simulation engine.

Our architecture is modular: the engine and ML pipelines are built to generalize across other complex, multi-agent competitive titles.
